# Me

I am a Developer Advocate at [DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com), where I help developers build and ship better apps on the cloud. I focus on creating [educational content](https://ajot.me/writing), speaking at events, and working closely with the developer community to improve their experience with DigitalOcean’s tools and services.

Previously, I’ve spent years advocating for developers at [Amazon Alexa](https://developer.amazon.com/en-GB/blogs/alexa/author.amit-jotwani), Intel Mashery, and [Retool](https://retool.com/blog/author/amit) — writing, speaking, and building resources to help them succeed.

By day, I break down complex ideas into simple, no-fluff [quickstart guides](https://ajot.me/posts/the-goal-of-a-tutorial-isnt-to-make-people-follow-every-step.). 

Compulsive Seinfeld quoter. LEGO nerd. I live in New York City with my wife and our ~~two~~ three-year-old daughter, who teaches me more about time management than any productivity book ever could!

Also: amateur stand-up comedian. My wife got tired of being my only audience and signed me up for comedy classes. Now, I occasionally tell sad jokes at Gotham and other NYC comedy clubs. She regrets this decision.

You can find me on Twitter at [@amit](https://twitter.com/amit), or see what I’m building at [curiousmints.com](https://curiousmints.com).
